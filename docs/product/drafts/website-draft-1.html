Questions

Fair questions about AI-built software.

Who is responsible if something goes wrong?

We are, the two developers. The AI does the building, but nothing counts as done until one of us has checked it in the running app and signed it off. You always know who to call.

Is code written by AI any good?

On its own, not reliably. That's why every feature passes several layers of automated tests, a separate AI review against our architecture rules, and a human check from a list written before the code existed. Code that fails any of them isn't delivered.

How fast is fast?

A focused MVP usually takes a few weeks. If your idea is bigger than that, we'll tell you at the first meeting and help you pick the first version that is worth building.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first day, including the tests and the documentation. If you ever want to continue with someone else, they can pick it up from where we left off.

Do we really meet in person?

Yes. We're local. We start at your place, and we come back whenever a conversation is better had face to face. Between meetings we keep you up to date and you can try the app yourself.

What kind of software do you build?

Web applications for small and medium-sized companies: customer portals, booking and planning systems, internal tools, dashboards, and apps for people working out in the field on a phone.

What happens after launch?

We can keep building at the same rate, or hand it over. The documentation is kept up to date the whole way, so either works.
